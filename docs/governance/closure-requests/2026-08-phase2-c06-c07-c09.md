## C-06 — identity aliases and collision controls

The five deterministic ADR-0020 collision cases are recorded in the
[Phase 2 vertical-slice evidence package](../../evidence/2026-08-02-phase2-vertical-slice.md):

- duplicate serial across sources;
- hostname reuse after validity expiry;
- IP movement between devices;
- confidence-tie quarantine; and
- merge lineage appended to both identities.

The governing identity decision is
[ADR-0020](../../adr/0020-identity-alias-conflict-resolution.md), with the
deterministic identity implementation covered by
[`tests/phase2/test_phase2_identity.py`](../../../tests/phase2/test_phase2_identity.py).

Status remains OPEN. Closure request: owner review and register-conformant disposition. This record does not close the condition.

## C-09 — connector polling and source-impact controls

The authoritative values are taken verbatim from
[ADR-0023](../../adr/0023-connector-polling-source-impact-controls.md#per-class-defaults):

| Source class | Min interval | Connect timeout | Read timeout |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Redfish BMC — health | 30 s | 5 s | 10 s |
| Redfish BMC — standard/inventory | 60–300 s | 5 s | 10 s |
| SNMP — critical counters | 30 s | — (UDP) | 5 s |
| SNMP — default/environmental | 60–300 s | — (UDP) | 5 s |

The new [connector ceiling and kill-switch tests](../../../tests/phase2/test_connector_ceilings.py)
cover Redfish 30 s / 10 s and SNMP 60 s / 5 s contract boundaries, tier-1
`enabled: false` zero-record behavior, tier-2 stop-file interruption and
resume on the same replay position, and the absence of write-style public
methods. Existing [Redfish read-only tests](../../../tests/phase2/test_redfish_adapter_readonly.py)
and [SNMPv3 read-only tests](../../../tests/phase2/test_snmpv3_adapter_readonly.py)
retain the no-network and no-control-path checks.

These adapters are synthetic replay iterators, not live pollers. Tier 3
orchestration stop (`SIGTERM`) with graceful drain of at most 10 s, and the
zero-request/source-side observation portions of the stop test, are deferred
to an integration-host acceptance run under ADR-0023. They are not tested-here
claims in this package.

Status remains OPEN. Closure request: owner review and register-conformant disposition. This record does not close the condition.
